IOTA · Beam dynamics

From pickup radiation to a corrective kick.

My current accelerator-physics work focuses on amplified optical stochastic cooling at the Integrable Optics Test Accelerator. The problem connects undulator radiation, optical transport, lattice design, and six-dimensional beam dynamics in a single feedback system.

I am studying how pickup-to-kicker transport, longitudinal and transverse coupling, optical amplification, and cooling decrements determine performance. Longer term, this framework opens questions around optical stochastic crystallization and phase-space configurations for coherent emission.

About the work

Research that moves between theory and implementation.

I work across accelerator physics, computational optics, and scientific machine learning. I am most interested in problems where a rigorous physical model can become a practical design, diagnostic, or control method.

My approach favors explicit assumptions, reproducible software, and close connections between simulation and experimental observables. Alongside research, I teach introductory physics and build tools that make technically dense work easier to inspect.
